# Search page: scope and visitor

Visitor: journalists and newsroom staff. Mode: Operate (find and open articles). Scope: web/app/page.tsx, the search surface.
Job: type a topic or phrase, scan dated results, open the source article.
Constraints: results are stored articles with source links only, no generated answers. Team login gate stays. Bangla and English. Light and dark. Phone width.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The archive is a newsroom index. Each result is a dated headline in the paper's own type, not a chat reply. Refuses: chat bubbles, an assistant voice, nested cards, a hero.

OWN-WORLD: Newsprint paper #f3f2ec, ink #15171c, hairline rules, one archive blue #1f3d7a for links and the active facet. Newsreader for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for metadata, Noto Serif Bengali for Bangla. Double rule under the masthead.

STORY: The visitor recalls a topic, types it, and sees a dated index of matching stories grouped by day. They scan headlines, check outlet and date, and open the source.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered masthead "The Archive". Search as a full-width underlined serif field, Enter submits. Below: facets left (outlet, filed under), results right grouped by day, newest first. Primary action: the search field.

FORM: Editorial index. Chosen by the user from three options (editorial archive, research console, search-first minimal). Direction pinned by the user, so the dice roll was skipped. Seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
